Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Moon's Kitchen ("we", "us") runs this website, moonskitchen.netlify.app. This page explains what information we collect when you use it and what we do with it.

Browsing and ordering

You can browse the site and order without creating an account. Orders are placed on WhatsApp, where WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to your messages.

Signing in (optional)

If you choose to sign in, we receive:

  • With Google: your name and email address, shared by Google with your permission. We do not receive your Google password or any other Google account data.
  • With an email code: the email address you enter. We send a one-time 6-digit code to it.

We use this only to keep you signed in and to recognise you on the site. We do not sell or share it with anyone for marketing.

Cookies

When you sign in we set one cookie, mk_session, which keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Logging out removes it. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies ourselves. The Instagram posts shown on our home page are loaded from Instagram, which may set its own cookies under its privacy policy.

Your basket, and the name, phone, address and pickup or delivery choice you enter on the Order page, are remembered in your own browser's local storage so they are there when you come back.

Orders placed on this website

You can place an order on the website when you are signed in. We then save your email, name, phone number, items, pickup or delivery choice, address, preferred date and notes, so we can bake and deliver your order and you can follow it under My orders. If you write a review, we save it with the name you choose and show it on the site once we have approved it. Orders sent on WhatsApp reach us only as the message you send.

Services we use

  • Netlify hosts the website and runs the sign-in.
  • Brevo sends the email sign-in codes and our own new-order emails.
  • Supabase stores orders and reviews placed on this website.
  • Google provides "Continue with Google" sign-in.
  • Instagram shows our videos and photos on the home page, loaded from Instagram's own servers.

These services process data only as needed to provide those features.

Your choices

You can log out at any time from the account menu at the top of the site. To ask about or remove any information we hold about you, email us at siddikroy1@gmail.com.
